Specialized Capabilities

Our core services focus on structural precision, thermal efficiency, and high-end finishing.

Framing

Expert Metal Stud Framing (Light Gauge Steel) for commercial and residential structures. We ensure structural integrity, millimeter precision, and code compliance for partitions, bulkheads, and load-bearing walls.

Insulation

Comprehensive thermal and sound insulation solutions. We install fiberglass, mineral wool, and rigid foam to maximize energy efficiency and acoustic performance in every project.

Drywall

High-end gypsum board installation and finishing (Level 3 to Level 5). We handle fire-rated assemblies, moisture-resistant boards, and complex architectural details with superior surface quality.

Acoustic Ceilings

Professional installation of Acoustical Ceiling Tiles (ACT) and grids. We deliver functional and aesthetic ceiling solutions that optimize sound control for offices, retail, and commercial spaces.
