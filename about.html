EST. 1998 • THE LEGACY OF PROJETA

Projeta USA was born from the solid foundations of Projeta Engenharia (Brazil).

For more than two decades, Projeta has supported complex industrial and infrastructure demands, building a strong technical archive and proven delivery methodology. Projeta USA carries this legacy forward — expanding internationally with a focus on quality, safety, and consistency.

Commitment to Quality and Continuous Improvement
